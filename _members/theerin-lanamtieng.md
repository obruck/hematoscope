Theerin is a hematologist at Khon Kaen University, Thailand, specializing in diagnosing and treating blood cancers. Challenges and limitations in his clinical practice inspired him to become a doctoral researcher, aiming to find innovative solutions to improve patient care. At the Hematoscope Lab, he focuses on research involving morphological analysis by artificial intelligence, aiming to uncover patterns in clinical and genetic data that can transform diagnostics and treatment.
