Yao holds an MSc in Statistics from Capital Normal University in Beijing, China. His research interests include transfer learning, high-dimensional statistical modelling, and heterogeneous data integration. At the Hematoscope Lab, his PhD project focuses on constructing synthetic control arms for acute myeloid leukemia (AML) and myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS) trials, examining normal blood cell morphology, and developing patient similarity network-based digital twins for risk stratification. He is interested in connecting statistical methodology with clinical research to make better use of biomedical data.
