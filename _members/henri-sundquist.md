Henri holds an MSc in Life Science Informatics from the University of Helsinki. Currently, he is pursuing a PhD in computational cytomorphology through the iCANDOC doctoral education pilot. Current projects include developing methods to phenotype single cells from MGG-stained BM slides and discovering biomarkers predicting venetoclax response in AML. With a background in computer science, he is interested in applying computational methods to medicine and the biological sciences.
