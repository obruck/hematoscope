Mikko is a doctoral student with an M.Sc. in Life Science Technologies from Aalto University. He specializes in machine and deep learning, and with an engineering mindset, he is passionate about applying theoretical knowledge to practical applications, particularly in the field of medicine. Mikko has been working on image analysis algorithms for the Hematoscope application and on research projects investigating bone marrow cytomorphology in leukemia patients. Additionally, he contributes to medical device regulation efforts to bring the Hematoscope application into clinical practice. Outside of work, Mikko enjoys endurance training in the outdoors.
