Welcome to join our team! We are open for applications from master's students, PhD students or researchers looking for work for a fixed-term or perhaps for longer. You would be working on cell classification algorithm to identify rare subtypes of hematopoietic cells. The work will consist of applying the PyTorch torchvision library. Our clinical team will prepare and and annotate the data for you. The work will have a tremendous impact on routine diagnostic practice. If interested, send your CV and brief motivation letter by clicking on the enveloppe at the bottom of the webpage. We are eager to discuss more!
