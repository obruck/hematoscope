Business Finland organized the Cancer Partnering Days event connecting academic research groups, biomedical SMEs and large pharmaceutical companies. The Hematoscope Lab represented the Helsinki University Hospital. Especially discussions with pharmaceutical companies were fruitful. These helped us to clarify our strategy and priorities to ensure that the Hematoscope App could be used by clinicians, researchers in academia and R&D divisions in pharmaceutical companies. Looking forward for potential collaborations with pharmaceutical companies!
